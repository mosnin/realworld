# Realworld

Realworld is a living network of digital missions where one to fifty humans and autonomous agents create useful things together.

A Mission begins as an intention. It develops a constitution, outcomes, branches, moves, artifacts, participants, memory, and a pulse of autonomous activity. People can start privately, invite collaborators, discover public Missions, answer a Call, fork an approach, or connect one Mission to another.

The primary interface is not chat or a task board. It is a spatial Field that makes the Mission's changing structure visible and directly manipulable. Users can zoom from the whole Mission into one outcome, artifact, participant, decision, or agent run.

## Product promise

Open Realworld and enter a world that has been thinking, building, and evolving in your absence.

## Non-negotiable qualities

- Useful alone and increasingly powerful with more people
- Actual digital output, not conversation about output
- Autonomous without becoming uncontrollable
- Social without requiring meetings
- Playful without hiding serious work behind gamification
- Understandable at a glance despite parallel activity
- Forkable, mergeable, replayable, and attributable
- Fast enough to feel alive

## Core loop

1. Seed a Mission with an outcome and boundaries.
2. The Steward proposes structure and creates the first useful Moves.
3. Humans and agents claim, delegate, and complete Moves.
4. Work produces versioned Artifacts and evidence.
5. Uncertainty creates Calls; disagreement can create Branches.
6. The Mission synthesizes progress and exposes Fractures.
7. Completed Proof unlocks the next meaningful state.
8. Participants invite others because the Mission now has visible momentum and specific needs.

## Network effects

The network grows through work, not contact importing:

- Missions recruit complementary contributors.
- Calls can be answered without joining permanently.
- Artifacts carry attributable lineage back to their Missions.
- Reusable capabilities improve participant and agent reputation.
- Missions can depend on, fork, merge with, or sponsor other Missions.
- Public Proof creates discovery and invites participation.
